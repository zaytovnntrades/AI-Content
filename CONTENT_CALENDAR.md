## Weekly workflow (batch it, don't post day by day)
- **Sunday, about 2 hours:** generate all of the week's voiceovers and visuals.
- **Monday, about 2 hours:** edit 4–5 Shorts in one CapCut session using one saved template (fonts, caption style, music level).
- **Daily, 10 minutes:** post, pin the comment, and reply to the first 10 comments within the first hour.

## Tips that matter most
1. **The hook is the whole game.** The first 2 seconds decide whether people swipe away. Every script above opens on a strong image and a strange fact, never "Hi guys."
2. **End on a question.** Comments help a video spread, so ask something people can answer in one word.
3. **Link Shorts together.** Use YouTube's "Related video" link on each Short to send viewers to Part 1, Part 2 or the next episode.
4. **Re-use everything.** Each Short also becomes a TikTok, an Instagram Reel, a quote card and a teaser re-cut. One script gives you 4–5 posts.
5. **After 7 days,** compare viewed-vs-swiped and average view duration in YouTube Studio. Make more of whichever philosopher or format wins.
