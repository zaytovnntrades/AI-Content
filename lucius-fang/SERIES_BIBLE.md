# LUCIUS FANG — *The Last Signal*
A 3-series animated cyberpunk saga · 15 episodes per series · ~60 s per episode · Seedance 2.0

---

## 1. Core concept
At age 6, Lucius Fang wakes in a silent, neon-soaked megacity. The grid is dead, the people are gone,
and the only "teacher" left is the world itself — ruins, libraries, machines, and the traces of
human action. He learns to survive alone, then learns *why* humans built anything at all, then
rebuilds knowledge in his own way.

**Theme ladder (one per series):**
| Series | Title | Lucius' age | Question the audience leaves with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **OFF GRID** | 6 → 12 | *What do you actually need to survive?* |
| 2 | **OPEN SOURCE** | 12 → 18 | *Who owns knowledge — and what happens when it's free?* |
| 3 | **THE MAKER** | 18 → 25 | *If you had to rebuild the world, what would you keep?* |

## 2. Style lock (paste into EVERY prompt — this is your consistency anchor)
> **STYLE KEY:** stylized 3D animated film, cyberpunk digital aesthetic, abandoned neon megacity,
> magenta and cyan rim light, holographic glitch particles, rain-slick reflections, volumetric fog,
> dust motes in light beams, cinematic anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, moody score.
> No other people. No text on screen.

**Character key (paste every time Lucius appears):**
> **LUCIUS:** East-Asian boy with messy black hair, one streak of electric-cyan hair, amber eyes,
> oversized patched charcoal hoodie, a cracked glowing wrist-band (his only "device"), scuffed boots.
> *(Series 2: teen, taller, goggles on forehead. Series 3: young man, cyber-prosthetic left hand he built himself.)*

**Companion:** **BYTE** — a small salvaged drone with one blue eye-light. Gives Lucius someone to "talk"
to without breaking the last-man-on-earth feel.

## 3. Episode formula (60 s = 4 × 15 s Seedance clips)
| Clip | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| A — HOOK | 0–15 s | Striking image + problem ("the water is gone") |
| B — STRUGGLE | 15–30 s | Lucius tries, fails, observes |
| C — LESSON | 30–45 s | He figures it out — the skill/knowledge lands |
| D — **THINK SHOT** | 45–60 s | **Cinematic pause**: slow push-in / wide aerial / time-lapse, one line of narration that asks the viewer a question |

The **THINK SHOT** is your signature. Always: slow camera, wide scale, silence + one sentence.
Example camera language: *"slow crane up revealing the endless empty city"*, *"extreme close-up
on his eye reflecting the city, rack focus"*, *"360° orbit, time freezes, rain hangs in the air"*.

## 8. Production plan & credit math (checked Oct 2026)
- Seedance 2.0, 15 s clip: **67.5 credits** (std, 720p) · **15 credits** (fast, 480p)
- 1 episode = 4 clips → **270** (std) or **60** (fast)
- Full saga = 45 episodes = 180 clips → **~12,150** (std) or **~2,700** (fast)

**High-ROI shortcut workflow:**
1. **Lock the look first** — generate one Lucius character image + one city keyframe (cheap images).
   Feed them as `image_references` into every Seedance clip → same boy, same world, every episode.
2. **Pilot, then scale** — make Ep 1.1 only, post it, measure retention, then batch the rest.
3. **Make the think shot count** — it's the clip people screenshot and share. Spend std/1080p
   credits there; use fast mode for action clips.
4. **Stitch** the 4 clips per episode (CapCut / Higgsfield montage), add one narration track and
   consistent music sting at the think shot = your "brand sound."

### EPISODE 1.3 — "Fire Without Power"
*Week 2. The first cold night. Lesson: fire is humanity's oldest technology, and you can make it from what others threw away.*

**Clip A: HOOK** · genre `drama` · extra ref: apartment `650a4b64-4617-40e2-81e5-5fc1bce2aa21`
> Night in the tiny apartment, frost creeping across the big window, the neon skyline outside dark. Lucius
> shivers under a thin blanket, breath fogging, his wristband the only light. He flicks a dead electric
> heater switch again and again: nothing. He hugs his knees. Slow push-in, blue cold color grade.
> Sound: howling wind, rattling window, chattering teeth, no voices.

**Clip B: STRUGGLE** · genre `drama` · extra ref: city `94b84c40-e610-436e-802a-3835ce3d091e`
> Lucius scavenges an abandoned electronics store with a flashlight beam: he snaps a dead lighter
> (no spark), rubs two plastic rods together (nothing), and burns his fingers on a sparking broken cable,
> jerking back. He sits frustrated among the junk, then spots a cracked magnifying lens and a shiny dead
> battery on the floor. Handheld camera, flickering emergency lights. Sound: clatter, sparking crackle, a small gasp.

**Clip C: LESSON** · genre `drama`
> Rooftop at sunrise. Lucius piles dry paper, cardboard and shredded cloth into a rusty metal drum. He
> holds the magnifying lens so a beam of pale sunlight focuses into one tiny bright point. Smoke curls up,
> he blows gently, and a small orange flame bursts to life. He feeds it carefully, warming his hands, the
> firelight painting his face gold against the magenta sky. Low-angle hero shot.
> Sound: crackling fire growing, warm hopeful synth swell.

**Clip D: THINK SHOT** · genre `epic`
> Extreme close-up of the flame dancing, reflected in Lucius' amber eyes. Slow pull-back as he sits
> beside the glowing fire drum on the rooftop at night. Slow crane up: his single warm orange light is the
> only fire in the entire dark, frozen megacity below. Near silence, crackling embers, a deep synth note.
> A soft child's voice narrates: "Fire was humanity's first technology. Today it was mine."

**Hidden lesson (description):** *A magnifying lens + dry tinder + direct sun = fire with no power at all. Always build fire in a metal container, away from anything that can catch.*
